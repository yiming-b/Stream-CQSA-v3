## The refit (job 14330294, 4 x A100 80GB PCIe, della-l02g13)

Model of a rank's local time, fitted by `dist_cost_refit.py` (the constants now in `scheduler.PATH_COSTS`,
`TASK_S`, `BWD_FACTOR`, `KNOWN_DEVICE_RATES`):

    t_local = sum over depth calls [ N * call_tok_s[path] ] + sum over units [ tiles*B*H*f_dir/rate + task_s + L*tok_s[path] ]

| constant | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| rate (PCIe-80, host-streamed fwd) | 2.912e7 tiles/s | the seed was 2.9e7; D1's startup calibration gave 2.35e7 because it subtracted only the launches |
| BWD_FACTOR | 3.64 | device-resident backward per live tile / forward (D1 assumed 3.0) |
| pool: call_tok_s | 6.46e-7 s/token of N | the shared chunk pool's H2D copy per engine call: 0.68 s at 1M, 1.36 s at 2M |
| pool: tok_s | 0 | in-place reads: nothing per gathered token |
| host: tok_s | 5.24e-7 s/gathered token | the host gather path (four ranks sharing one host): 0.10 s per 192K-token child |
| host: call_tok_s | 1.85e-7 s/token of N | per gather-path call (0.19 s at 1M) |
| dev: tok_s, call_tok_s | 0, 0 | device-resident inputs: nothing beyond the launch |
| task_s | 3 ms | held at the single-device fit (free: fits to 0, nothing else moves) |
| throttled rank (54/108 SMs) | 0.573 of a full rank | |
| residual | rms 2.0%, slowest rank <= 3.9% per arm | 136 observations |

Why D1's model missed by 1.09-1.49x: it had no per-call term (the pool copy alone is 18% of a 1M itr=1
rank's time and is paid once per depth, so a split rank pays it twice) and 20x too small a per-token cost
on the gather path (2.5e-8 vs 5.2e-7: with four ranks on one host the gather is not hidden).

Merge (critical path, `t_dist - max t_local`, per token of B*H*D=512 per reduced tensor, world 4):
allreduce 0.95e-7 s (fwd 1M 0.12-0.13 s, 2M 0.21-0.24 s), reduce_scatter sharded 0.80e-7 s (1M 0.09-0.10 s,
2M 0.17-0.20 s), backward 3x (three gradients); fixed 0.03 s. `CostModel.dist_allreduce_tok_s`,
`dist_reduce_scatter_tok_s`, `dist_all_gather_tok_s` (0.6e-7, a seed: the replicated result after a
reduce_scatter was not measured; the het job's `/rep` arm measures it), `dist_fixed_s`, scaled by the ring
volume (W-1)/W and the link relative to the nominal 25 GB/s the measured ring's 26 GB/s matches.

## The split rule

The loads carry the split's price (c-1 launches, the children's gathered tokens, the second depth call), so
the lexicographic acceptance of D1 already prices it; what was missing is that an equal-makespan step (a
split whose children land on the lightest ranks) was kept as progress. It still is explored (7 tasks on 8
ranks need it), but the result stands only if the final makespan is more than `min_gain` = 2% below the
unsplit one; otherwise the whole units (round-robin's partition up to the pairing of equal tasks) stand.
On the real 1M task lists (`tests/test_ultimate_scheduler.py::test_split_rule_reproduces_the_measured_winners`):

| regime | rule | measured (job 14330294) |
|---|---|---|
| fwd 1M itr=1, equal ranks, host-streamed | whole tasks 2 2 2 1 (0 splits), 3.32 s | rr 3.51-3.55 s beat the 6 6 6 7 split 3.94-3.95 s |
| fwd 2M itr=1 | whole tasks, 11.90 s | rr 12.29 vs split 12.46 (tie) |
| fwd itr=2 | balanced whole-task LPT 12 12 12 13, ratio 1.000 | lpt 3.94 vs rr 4.05-4.11 |
| bwd 1M itr=1, device-resident | 3 splits, 6 6 6 7, 8.42 s | lpt 8.53 vs rr 9.85-10.26; the same assignment |
| fwd 1M itr=1, rank 1 at 0.573 | whole tasks 2 1 2 2, 3.32 s | lpt/measured 3.52 vs rr 5.48 |
| fwd 1M itr=2, rank 1 at 0.573 | 14 9 13 13 | lpt/measured 13 9 14 13: 4.32 vs rr 5.47 |

## The planner

`CostModel.cqsa_time(world>1)` = `scheduler.dist_local_time(n_tasks, rel_rates, unit_s=kernel/n_tasks, L, N,
paths, task_s)` + `dist_merge_s` + `dist_fixed_s`: the slowest rank's local time by the driver's own scheduler
on equal subproblems of the planner's per-subproblem kernel time with the refit per-unit/per-call costs (which
replace the single-device host terms: they were fitted with four ranks on one host). The 1.35 imbalance factor
is gone (the per-rank residual is 1.00 +- 0.04). Consequence for `tests/test_devkit_autoconfig.py::
test_planner_uses_devices_when_faster`: 2 devices at c=7, 4M, device-resident are planned distributed (68 vs
95 s monolithic): 4/7 of the itr=1 pair work at 1.4x FA-2's cost per pair is 0.8x the monolithic time. The
assertion encoded the pre-D1 driver's host-accumulator measurement (34.6 vs 24.2 s mono at 2M, LOG.md job
13763575), which the model does not reproduce (20 s predicted) and which has not been re-measured with the
D1/D2 driver: a host-accumulator multi-GPU row is the open point. The planner's single-device time is
SXM-calibrated (8.4 s predicted vs 10.3 s measured at 1M itr=1 on PCIe-80), so its absolute world>1 numbers
inherit that -10% on this node; the scheduler model (tile rate) is the validated one.

## Open issues

* `dist_all_gather_tok_s` (the replicated result after a reduce_scatter, the driver's default) is a seed;
  the het job's `lpt/reduce_scatter/measured/rep` arm measures it against `lpt/allreduce/measured`. If the
  all_gather makes reduce_scatter+replicated slower than allreduce, the default should become
  `allreduce` for replicated results and `reduce_scatter` for sharded ones.
* The host path's per-token cost was fitted with four ranks on one host (gather bandwidth shared); a 2-rank
  node or a host with more cores would pay less. The device path has no per-token term at all.
* A host-accumulator (`acc="cpu"`) multi-GPU row has not been measured with the D1/D2 driver; the planner
  adds the single-device unhidden host-accumulator stages times the slowest rank's share.
